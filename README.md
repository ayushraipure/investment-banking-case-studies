# JPMorgan Chase – Fraud Detection Operations Case Study

## Overview

This case study explores a hypothetical fraud detection and transaction monitoring scenario within banking operations.

The objective is to understand how suspicious transactions can be identified, investigated, documented, and escalated while maintaining operational accuracy and compliance with internal controls.

> **Note:** This is an educational case study created for learning and portfolio purposes. It is not based on confidential JPMorgan Chase data or internal systems.

## Business Problem

Financial institutions process a large volume of transactions every day. Detecting unusual or potentially fraudulent activity requires effective transaction monitoring, alert investigation, risk assessment, and escalation procedures.

The case study focuses on how an operations team can identify suspicious transaction patterns and take appropriate action according to defined procedures.

## Objectives

* Identify potentially suspicious transactions.
* Analyze transaction patterns and unusual activity.
* Understand the fraud-alert investigation process.
* Assess potential operational and compliance risks.
* Determine appropriate escalation steps.
* Improve accuracy and consistency in transaction monitoring.

## Approach

The analysis follows a structured operational workflow:

1. Transaction monitoring
2. Alert identification
3. Initial transaction review
4. Customer and transaction-level analysis
5. Risk assessment
6. Supporting information review
7. Documentation of findings
8. Escalation where required
9. Closure or further investigation

## Key Areas Analyzed

### Transaction Monitoring

Transactions are reviewed for unusual patterns such as:

* Unusual transaction amounts
* Unexpected transaction frequency
* Sudden changes in transaction behavior
* Multiple transactions within a short period
* Transactions inconsistent with expected activity

### Alert Investigation

Potential alerts are reviewed using available transaction and customer information to determine whether the activity requires further investigation or escalation.

### Risk Assessment

The investigation considers factors such as transaction characteristics, unusual activity patterns, customer information, and relevant risk indicators.

### Escalation

Where activity requires further review, the case is documented and escalated through the appropriate operational or compliance workflow.

## Operational Controls

The case study highlights the importance of:

* Accurate data review
* Proper documentation
* Following standard operating procedures
* Maker-checker controls where applicable
* Timely escalation
* Data confidentiality
* Consistent investigation processes
